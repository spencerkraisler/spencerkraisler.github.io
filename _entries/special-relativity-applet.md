Decades ago I played a Flash game that did something similar to this. I can't find it anymore, so I remade it.

I've been reading a lot about special relativity lately and having a blast. I hope to write a blog entry about it soon. I was able to come up with a very simple and beautiful derivation of Lorentz boost from the same 2 principles Einstein started off with:
1. The speed of light is constant in all inertial frames of reference
2. Principle of Relativity: laws of physics are the same in all inertial frames (and any law that isn't the same, like $F=ma$ is incorrect)
