What is being integrated

The configuration is g ∈ SO(3) for the body and the wheel angle φ relative to the body. The group is SO(3) and the shape space is the circle. Here I is the locked inertia (body plus wheel, wheel not spinning), Iw is the wheel’s axial inertia, and â is the wheel axis in the body frame.
